How it works

From reference to reflex.

A straightforward way to learn, practice, and build speed without navigating complicated menus.

01

Browse A to Z

See every NATO code word in one scrollable reference. Tap a word to hear its pronunciation.

02

Train hands-free

Tap to speak once. Say each code word aloud and the app listens again as you move through the letters.

03

Put yourself to the test

Use Practice mode to learn at your own pace or Challenge mode for a 26-letter test. Track progress on your device.
